3. Import database/siper_perpus.sql melalui HeidiSQL/phpMyAdmin
4. Buka http://localhost/SIPER_Perpus/login.php
5. Login: admin/password, petugas/password, anggota/password
6. Baca README.md, docs/ANALISIS.md, docs/TESTING.md, docs/BUG_LOG.md dan docs/DEMO_5-7_MENIT.md
